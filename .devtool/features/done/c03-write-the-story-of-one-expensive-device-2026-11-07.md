# C03 · Write the “story” of one expensive device

**Time:** ~30 min  ·  **Do first:** C02

**Why:** Judges love one concrete example. This becomes our demo storyline.

## Steps
1. Pick one device from C02 that is *not* biowaste.
2. Find: its material stream, collection policy, number of lifts, average fill before lift, operator.
3. Write a short story: “Device X at site Y was emptied N times at only Z % full, costing € … per tonne vs a benchmark of € …”.
4. Label every number as **observed** or **estimated**.

## Done when
- [x] The story (≤ 6 sentences) is in FINDINGS.md.
