# U12 · Pick our weighing accuracy target

**Time:** ~20 min  ·  **Do first:** U10, U11

**Why:** Connects the hardware idea (U11) with the money it unlocks (U10).

## Steps
1. With the team, choose one concept from U11.
2. Pick a realistic accuracy (e.g. 1.5 %) and read its row from the U10 table.
3. Write 3 bullets: concept, accuracy, what that accuracy lets the customer check.

## Done when
