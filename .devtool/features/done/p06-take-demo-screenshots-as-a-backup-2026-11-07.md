# P06 · Take demo screenshots as a backup

**Time:** ~10 min  ·  **Do first:** C13

**Why:** If the live demo breaks on stage, screenshots save us.

## Steps
1. Run the app, pick the C03 device.
2. Screenshot every part of the screen.
3. Put them on slide 3 (hidden or as backup) and in `notes/screenshots/`.

## Done when
- [x] At least 3 screenshots are saved.

## Result
- 7 screenshots in `notes/screenshots/` (taken headless with Playwright): story device charts and costs, weight checks for CMP-00042 and CMP-00047, proposals with one approved, full page. Two backup slides in the deck appendix.
