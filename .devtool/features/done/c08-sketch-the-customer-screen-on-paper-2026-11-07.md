# C08 · Sketch the customer screen on paper

**Time:** ~30 min  ·  **Do first:** C03

**Why:** Before coding the demo, agree on what the customer actually sees.

## Steps
1. On paper or in Excalidraw/Figma, draw one screen with: device picker, fill + weight chart, € per tonne, list of flagged issues, a button “Approve proposal”.
2. Show it to 2 teammates and adjust.
3. Take a photo and add it to `notes/`.

## Done when
- [x] The sketch is in `notes/` and the team agreed on it.

## Help
- Every proposal must need **human approval**. Show that on the screen.

## Result
- `notes/customer_screen_sketch.png` (wireframe drawn as SVG instead of a paper photo). The built app follows it: device picker → fill and weight charts → € per tonne → lifts with weight source → weight check → proposals with Approve / Reject / Undo. Human approval is visible on the screen.
