# B02 · Brainstorm 5 service ideas and vote on one

**Time:** ~30 min  ·  **Do first:** B01, C03

**Why:** We need one clear business model to pitch.

## Steps
1. Everyone writes 2 ideas on sticky notes (e.g. invoice-check service, monthly weight report, operator tendering help, per-tonne full service).
2. Group the duplicates and keep the best 5.
3. Dot-vote: each person gets 3 dots.
4. Write the winner + 1 sentence “who pays and why” in FINDINGS.md.

## Done when
- [ ] One chosen idea is written down.

## Help
- Ideas where Europress is *in the middle* of the value chain are fine but must be labelled **hypothesis**.
