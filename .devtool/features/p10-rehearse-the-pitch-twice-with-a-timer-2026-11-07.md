# P10 · Rehearse the pitch twice with a timer

**Time:** ~30 min  ·  **Do first:** P03, P04, P05, P06

**Why:** Practice is the cheapest way to win points.

## Steps
1. Ask the organisers for the pitch time limit.
2. Run through the whole pitch with the timer, live demo included.
3. Cut whatever goes over time. Rehearse again.

## Done when
- [ ] Two full runs finished within the time limit.
