# P06 · Take demo screenshots as a backup

**Time:** ~10 min  ·  **Do first:** C13

**Why:** If the live demo breaks on stage, screenshots save us.

## Steps
1. Run the app, pick the C03 device.
2. Screenshot every part of the screen.
3. Put them on slide 3 (hidden or as backup) and in `notes/screenshots/`.

## Done when
- [ ] At least 3 screenshots are saved.
