# Part 1

## 1. Weight cells 
Europress would integrate only to some containers weight cells and rotate them by periods of times between customers.

Then, they'd collect data about the container pressure, waste category and it's weight to train a model which could then predict basing on the customer and it's location the exact weight of the container basing only on the pressure measurments.

Maybe this is even doable without ML. Plain customer profiles combined with different waste categories could be quite easy to estimate.
